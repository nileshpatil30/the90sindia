# 90s India — content backlog

A researched shortlist for filling out `js/config.js`. Each entry gives the
year, the network or studio that made it, and **who to check on YouTube**.

## Read this first: none of these carry a video ID

Every suggestion below has a deliberately blank `video` field. The IDs could
not be verified while this list was written, and this project's rule — set out
in the README — is to leave a field blank rather than guess. A wrong ID ships a
dead or mislabelled embed, gets DMCA-claimed, and is the sort of thing that
gets nostalgia sites rejected from AdSense.

To fill one in, use the RSS trick already documented in the README:

```
https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UCxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
```

A channel's own feed only ever lists that channel's uploads, so anything in it
is genuinely theirs. Confirm the channel matches the rights holder named below,
then paste the ID into `js/config.js`.

**Rights holders worth knowing.** Doordarshan for DD-era programming; Zee, Sony
and Star for their own 90s shows; Yash Raj, Dharma, Rajshri, Ultra and Shemaroo
for film; Sony Music India, Saregama, T-Series, Tips, Venus and Universal for
music; ICC and BCCI for cricket; WWE for wrestling.

A caution on the TV listings: several 90s serials have **no official upload at
all**, and some sit behind OTT licensing rather than YouTube. If you cannot find
a legitimate source, leave the entry as a placeholder card — that is a fair use
of the space and better than an embed that dies.

## Sections the site does not have yet

Ideas that need no video at all, so they are cheap to build and hard to break:

- **The tuck-shop shelf.** Rasna, Gold Spot, Campa Cola, Thums Up, Maggi,
  Phantom sweet cigarettes, Poppins.
- **Reading pile.** Amar Chitra Katha, Tinkle, Champak, Chacha Chaudhary,
  Nandan, Chandamama.
- **Ad jingles everyone can still recite.** Nirma, Liril, Hamara Bajaj,
  Doordarshan's own logo animation.
- **The gadget shelf.** The "TV game" and its cartridges, cassette decks and
  the pencil rewind, Walkmans, landlines with a lock on the dial.
- **Trump cards.** WWF and cricket card decks — a natural fit for the Photo
  Wall.

Written as text cards, these sections carry the nostalgia without a single
rights question. Given how much of the video content above has licensing
trouble, they may end up the strongest part of the site.
