About 90s Rewind TV

90s Rewind TV is a fan-made hangout for 1990s India: TV shows, movies, classic ads, songs, cricket, WWF and games, all in one place. It is made by the people behind the @the90sindia Instagram page.

  • Live TV runs a fixed weekly timetable in Indian time, so everyone sees the same show at the same moment. Some timings are approximate and marked so in the guide.
  • Videos play in YouTube’s own embedded player. We do not download, copy or re-host anything. Official uploads come first. Where there is none, a community upload is used and labelled as one.
  • The Arcade lists original classic games and says honestly where each can be played. No clones, no ROMs.
  • Polls, trivia and memories work in your own browser for now. Nothing you type there is sent anywhere.

90s Rewind TV is a fan site. It is not affiliated with Doordarshan, any broadcaster, studio, sports body or game company. All shows, films, songs, brands and games belong to their owners.

Submit a Playlist or Video

Know a 90s show, ad, song or match that is on YouTube? Send us the link. We have no sign-up system yet, so submissions go through GitHub: this button opens a ready-filled message on our GitHub page (you need a free GitHub account to send it). Nothing is sent until you press the button there.

Only YouTube links are accepted. We check every submission for embedding and for official sources before adding it.

Community Guidelines

The community pages (forum, memories, polls) are for people who love the 90s. Please:

  • Be kind. No abuse, hate, bullying or personal attacks, including about actors, players and other fans.
  • Keep it 90s. Stay on topic: shows, films, ads, songs, cricket, toys, games and growing up then.
  • No piracy. Do not post links to pirated films, shows or game files (ROMs). We will remove them.
  • No spam or selling. No advertising, referral links or repeated posts.
  • Protect privacy. Do not share phone numbers, addresses or photos of people who did not agree.
  • Give credit. Say where a photo or story came from.

Breaking these rules means the post is removed, and repeat offenders lose posting access once accounts exist.
