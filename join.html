What it takes to turn accounts on safely

This site is a set of plain web pages with no server of its own, so it cannot keep a password safely. Real accounts need these pieces first:

  • A sign-in service. A hosted service (for example Supabase, Firebase or Auth0) keeps the accounts and stores each password as a salted hash, never as the password itself. We should not write our own.
  • Email confirmation and password reset, handled by that service, so nobody can sign up with someone else’s address.
  • HTTPS everywhere and rate limiting, so passwords travel encrypted and guessing is blocked.
  • A privacy policy and an age rule, because members’ email addresses become personal data. The form above already asks for 13 or older.
  • A database for member posts if the forum and shared memories are to be visible to everyone, with a way to report and remove posts.

Passwords are never saved in your browser by this page, and the fields above are cleared as soon as you press a button. The full comparison of options and costs is in docs/backend-options.md in the project.
